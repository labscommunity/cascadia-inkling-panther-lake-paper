This repository is a private author-review research artifact. It does not apply a new blanket public distribution license to the manuscript, generated figures or archived measurements. A public release license can be selected by the authors when releasing the artifact.

The paper is authored by Tate Berenbaum, Matias Parij and Muthaiah Venkatachalam. Affiliations and email addresses follow the companion Cascadia manuscripts. This attribution does not assert employer endorsement.

The frozen Cascadia code and documentation originate from `labscommunity/cascadia` at the commit in `reproduction/evidence/manifest.json`; they retain their existing notices and Apache-2.0 terms. A copy of the source license is included at [reproduction/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0](reproduction/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0). Data/output samples are retained as study evidence, not represented as newly authored code.

The local `arxiv.sty` was copied from the companion Cascadia architecture paper. Its existing style header is preserved. The new manuscript takes inspiration from the companion papers' LaTeX presentation and claim-to-evidence organization; their other experimental claims are not imported as results of this fleet.

Bibliographic sources retain their original rights and terms. The repository includes citations and original summaries, not full third-party papers or model weights. No license to Inkling weights is granted by this repository; consult the model author's current terms for those assets.
